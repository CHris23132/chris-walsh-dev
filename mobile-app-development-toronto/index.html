The questions people ask before booking a call.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on platforms and features. After the strategy call you get a timeline tied to a defined first-release feature list, including time for App Store and Google Play review.

How fast can we start?

Quickly. Book a strategy call and we can usually talk within a few days. If we’re a fit, you get a written scope and estimate shortly after, and work starts as soon as you approve it — no long sales process.

Do I need an iPhone app, an Android app, or both?

It depends on your customers. Cross-platform with React Native reaches both from one codebase; native makes sense when you need maximum performance or platform-specific features. We decide this on the strategy call.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes. I handle App Store and Google Play submission, store listing requirements and release, and work through any review feedback with you.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You own the code and the repository, and accounts are set up in your name. You are never locked in to working with me.

How do we work together day to day?

You work directly with me — no account manager and no agency handoff. You get regular progress updates, working builds to review, and quick answers when decisions come up.

What if I only need a small feature, not a full platform?

That’s fine — not every project is a full build. A single feature, an integration, a fix or a review of existing code can be scoped and quoted on its own. The strategy call is free either way, so it costs nothing to ask.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. I’m happy to sign your NDA before you share details — send it over before or after booking. Your idea and information are kept confidential either way.

What does something like this cost?

Typical mobile app projects start at $20K. Platforms, payments, real-time features and the backend are the biggest drivers. You get a clear estimate after the strategy call.
