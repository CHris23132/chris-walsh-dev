The questions people ask before booking a call.

How long does a typical project take?

Theme changes and small features move quickly; custom apps and integrations take longer. You get a timeline after the strategy call, tied to a defined scope.

Will working on my store affect live sales?

No. Work happens on a duplicate theme or a development store, and changes go live only after you have reviewed them.

Do you build custom Shopify apps or just themes?

Both. Theme work handles storefront changes; a custom app is the right call when you need new functionality, admin tools or integrations that existing apps don’t cover.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You own the code and the repository, and accounts are set up in your name. You are never locked in to working with me.

How do we work together day to day?

You work directly with me — no account manager and no agency handoff. You get regular progress updates, working builds to review, and quick answers when decisions come up.

What does something like this cost?

Typical Shopify projects run $5K–$20K. Custom apps, integrations and complex pricing logic are the biggest drivers. You get a clear estimate after the strategy call.
